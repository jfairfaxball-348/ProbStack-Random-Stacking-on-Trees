## Constant-cost regeneration

Yes: constant-cost regeneration exists.

After the Session 4 reset, if the outgoing integer message M satisfies
M<=-mu, it is already a deep seed.

Otherwise

-mu < M <= 2mu.

For one fresh geometric occupancy X, require

2mu+2-M <= X <= 4mu-M.

Then the exact effective value lies in [2mu+2,4mu], and the exact TreeStack
transfer lies in [mu,2mu].

The exact conditional probability is

r^(2mu+2-M) [1-r^(2mu-1)],

where r=mu/(mu+1). It is minimized at M=-mu+1, giving

delta_mu
=
r^(3mu+1) [1-r^(2mu-1)].

For all mu>=16,

delta_mu >= 8/(17e^3) > 0.0234.

The steering occupancy is at most 5mu-1. Thus the regeneration has one
coordinate, O(mu) mass, and no linear-in-L logarithmic cost.

## Spatial front coefficient

Concatenate:

1. the constant-probability reset;
2. the regeneration step unless the reset is already deep;
3. the sharp Session 7 local 0-to-0 excursion;
4. the constant-probability runaway buffer.

The reset, regeneration and runaway factors contribute only O(1) to the
negative base-2 logarithm.

Therefore the best state-independent spatial deep-front coefficient agrees
with the local one-sided coefficient in the limiting linear sense:

2 log_2 theta - 2 log_2(3e).

In the natural variable x=log_2 mu, the spatial rate is

x^2 + 2x log_2 x - 2 log_2(3e)x + o(x)

at the precision needed for every fixed global offset.

There is no remaining half-log-three penalty.

## Conditioning precision

The regeneration event is adaptive in the incoming message, so Session 8 does
not assume conditioned block independence.

Under the product law, each reserved superblock has a uniform conditional
hazard whenever the current prefix mass is at most 2nmu. On the event that the
whole product mass is at most 2nmu, this condition holds at every attempted
block.

Hence the probability of B consecutive failures together with total mass at
most 2nmu is at most (1-p)^B.

Conditioning on total mass exactly nmu then costs division by the exact
negative-binomial point probability.

At a fixed negative offset from the sharp center, Bp is
2^{Theta(sqrt(log_2 n))} up to polylogarithmic factors, so the failure
probability exp(-Bp) dominates the reciprocal conditioning cost
exp(O(log n)).

If desired, the Session 6 reset/runaway truncations plus the fixed-delay
Session 7 lower event give deterministic superblock mass O(mu log n); the new
steering coordinate itself is bounded by 5mu-1.
